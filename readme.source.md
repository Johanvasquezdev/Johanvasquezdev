## About

I'm a software engineering student at ITLA who enjoys building complete applications and understanding how the pieces fit together, from APIs and data models to deployment. I care about clean architecture, useful products, and reliable systems.

**Most used:** C# and TypeScript. **Currently learning:** Go and Flutter.

## What I'm building

- **[Armelis](https://github.com/Johanvasquezdev/Armelis):** Application security intelligence for AI-assisted teams.
- **[Meridas](https://github.com/Johanvasquezdev/Meridas-Assistant):** A proprietary operational evidence intelligence system that checks answers against source documents and surfaces conflicts for human review.
- **[MedAgenda](https://github.com/Johanvasquezdev/MedAgenda):** A healthcare scheduling system for patients, doctors, and administrators.
- **[Artemis Banking](https://github.com/Johanvasquezdev/ArtemisBankingPro):** A .NET banking application built with Clean Architecture.
- **AegisSpace:** A multi-cloud change observatory focused on cloud changes, Terraform drift, and AI agent evaluation.
- **Ossandra:** An operational intelligence platform connecting business operations with observability, security, analytics, and AI.
- **PyroGravity (Flame in Freefall):** A NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 team prototype exploring combustion in microgravity. I'm building the AI analysis component.
